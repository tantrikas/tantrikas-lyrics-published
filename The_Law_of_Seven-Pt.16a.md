# The Law of Seven-Pt.16a

*Master Key System --- Part 16a*

The Law of Seven turns the wheel\
Cycles move through all we feel\
Birth and growth and fruit decline\
Period and rhythm mark the time

Seven days complete the week\
Sevenfold changes rise and peak\
Moon and sound and light obey\
Heat and current find their way

Strength prepares the wheel again\

Higher law can turn the day
